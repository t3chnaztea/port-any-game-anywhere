## Optional tools

The source-authored browser example pins TypeScript7.0.2 under Apache-2.0 in its npm lockfile. Optional author checks pin Playwright1.64.1 under Apache-2.0 and PyYAML6.0.3 under MIT. Those packages are downloaded for development. They are not bundled in the skill archive. Their package notices remain with their installed copies.

Codex, Claude Code, Ghidra, Java, compilers, debuggers, Emscripten, emulators, Node, Python, browsers, and device firmware have their own terms and licenses. The skill does not bundle them or automatically install them. Naming a tool is not an endorsement or a compatibility guarantee.

Ghidra is a program-analysis tool used in the planned Linux x86-64 recovery route. A decompiler attempts to turn machine instructions into higher-level source. Its output may need substantial interpretation and correction. It does not establish the right to redistribute the original program or the recovered output.

## Asset policy

Do not add proprietary game data to the repository, test fixtures, issue attachments, or release archives. Prefer original fixtures for public demonstrations. Where user-supplied assets are required, record their required identity without publishing their contents.

Before adding a third-party file, record its source URL, revision, license, attribution, and redistribution requirements. Check the exact files being added. Keep those notices beside the affected material. A generated screenshot, trace, or extracted data file may still contain restricted material.
